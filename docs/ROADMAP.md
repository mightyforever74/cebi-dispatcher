Next: Sıra 4.3 (`GET /api/locations/latest` initial-state snapshot). --- ## Strategic Positioning **TulipFleet Enterprise Dispatcher** is the dispatch-and-routing layer of the TulipFleet fleet management platform. Its strategic position is: - **Flagship integrated product** for prospects who have not yet committed to a telematics vendor — TulipFleet hardware + dispatcher + mobile app as a single platform.- **Open ingestion layer** for prospects with existing third-party telematics (Geotab, Verizon Connect, Samsara, etc.) — onboarded via customer-funded connectors per [ADR-0007](docs/adr/ADR-0007-telematics-integration-strategy.md).- **NL-specialized compliance moat** for the regulatory environment forming around Vrachtwagenheffing (Temmuz 2026) and Zero-Emission Zones (18 cities, 2025–2026 rollout) — see [ADR-0012](docs/adr/ADR-0012-nl-compliance-layer.md) for the 3-phase Internal → Premium → Standalone evolution.- **Vertical focus:** Dutch and broader European mid-market logistics — **K/M-segment 3PLs with 50–300 vehicles** per the Top 100 Logistiek Dienstverleners 2025 segmentation (e.g., Dobbe Transport, TMA Logistics, De Dekker, Deudekom). G-segment (Neele-Vat, 1000+ employees) is a telemetry data customer, not a dispatcher target.- **Workaround-aware design** — Sectoral research (Onfleet, Bringg user adoption blogs, DoorDash dispatcher AI rollout case study) ve TulipFleet dispatcher persona analizi gösteriyor: planner'lar gerçek workflow'da Excel'e döner, telefonla rota değiştirir, AI recommendation'ı ignore eder. TulipFleet bunu bir hata değil, bir **integration scenario** olarak görür. UI/UX disipline'i: (1) Tabular View ağırlıklı bulk editing (Excel-like), (2) her ekranda Manual Override butonu, (3) Export/Import dostu data flow. Tam otonom dispatch (Mode 3) NL sendikaları + dispatcher güveni nedeniyle **explicit out-of-scope** kabul edilir (§Non-Goals). The product is positioned **"TulipFleet — works best with TulipFleet"** in marketing and sales. Third-party compatibility is a sales-conversation answer, not a homepage message. NL compliance is positioned as **operational necessity for the Dutch market**, not a marketing differentiator — until the standalone Compliance-as-a-Service product is launched in Phase 3. ### Strategic insights guiding Phase 2 prioritization (per ADR-0011 §10) - **KOBİ digital readiness gap.** evofenedex research: 80% of Dutch SMEs are not "digital ready." Competitors (Routific, OptimoRoute) avoid this segment due to complexity. TulipFleet's integrated single-vendor onboarding (hardware + mobile + dispatcher) reduces the barrier — this is a differentiator, not a problem.- **Ketenregie gap.** Sector sources (TLN, evofenedex) identify supply chain orchestration ("ketenregie") as the underserved capability in the SME segment. Large 3PLs have in-house systems; SMEs have none. Dispatcher's Mode 1 (Advisory) + Mode 2 (Exception-based) maturity model from [ADR-0005](docs/adr/ADR-0005-dispatch-workflow.md) is purpose-built for this gap.- **Compliance moat.** No consolidated "single data point" exists in the Netherlands for ZE-Zone, ontheffing, or Vrachtwagenheffing rules. TulipFleet's Phase 1 internal compliance layer becomes the seed for a Phase 3 standalone B2B data product (per ADR-0012).- **Advisory effort allocation.** Sectoral pattern extraction (2026-05-30) ve dispatcher persona reality check sonrası karar: solo dev engineering effort'unun **%70'i Advisory Mode UI ve Manual Override esnekliğine** yatırılır. Otonomlaştırma (Mode 2/3) yerine **planner'ın elini güçlendiren** araçlar (manual replan, bulk update, override toggle, tabular view) önceliklidir. ADR-0005 Mode 1 → Mode 2 evolution path korunur, ancak runtime effort dağılımı Mode 1 UI olgunluğuna yönelir.
